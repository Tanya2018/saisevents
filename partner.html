How we work

From your brief to your show floor

  1. You send your briefBuyer profile, sectors, numbers and dates. We reply within 48 hours.
  2. We source and vetWe search our network across Africa and the Middle East and screen every candidate against your criteria.
  3. We handle the paperworkApplications, matchmaking profiles, invitation letters and visas, on your timeline.
  4. We prepare every delegateA briefing on your programme rules and the matchmaking app, plus a WhatsApp support group during the show.
  5. We report backAttendance, meetings held and buyer feedback, sent to you after the show.
