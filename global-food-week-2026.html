Special feature

The first Abu Dhabi Honey Symposium

ADAFSA is running the inaugural Honey Symposium inside Global Food Week, together with Apimondia, the international federation of beekeepers. The theme is sustainable beekeeping in arid environments, and it opens on 6 October with the session on bee conservation and sustainable beekeeping.

If you trade in honey, this is a room worth being in. Kenyan and Tanzanian honey sits well against Gulf demand, and the buyers, packers and regulators are all in the same hall for three days.

  • Scientific programmeBee conservation, sustainable practice and bee product research

Questions

What buyers ask us

Straight answers.

Why do I need a code to open the registration link?

We screen buyers before they reach the form. It keeps our delegation list clean with the organiser, and it means the applications we send through get accepted. Ask Kate and we will issue yours.

I do not have a company website or profile. Can I still apply?

Not without one. It is mandatory. We build company profiles and simple websites for buyers who need them, so email or WhatsApp us and we will sort it before your application goes in.

What does SAIS Events charge me?

Tell us on WhatsApp and we will give you the figure before you commit to anything. There is no charge for finding out whether you qualify.

Can I bring a colleague?

The funded seat is issued to one named decision maker. A second person can travel with the delegation at their own cost, and we handle their booking and invitation letter too.

Am I obliged to buy anything at the show?

No. You are obliged to attend the 20 meetings booked for you. What you buy after that is your business.

Is Abu Dhabi worth it for an East African buyer?

The Gulf is where East African produce meets Gulf money, and Global Food Week puts dates, honey, agritech, retail and hospitality buying in the same halls. Last edition signed AED 6.6 billion in deals across 58 agreements.
