Who We Are

The regional bridge between
East Africa and global trade

SAIS Events is headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, with an active network across six East African countries and international connections spanning China, UAE, India, Egypt, USA, Turkey, South Asia, and Europe.

We were born from a simple observation: East African professionals with real purchasing power and genuine sourcing mandates had no credible, structured route into the world's leading international trade programmes. The gap wasn't ambition. It wasn't budget. It was access.

Today, we are East Africa's dedicated trade delegation coordinator — sourcing, vetting, briefing, and delivering qualified buyers and decision-makers to international expos and hosted buyer programmes where real deals get done.

How We Work

A clear path
from interest to impact

Every delegation follows the same disciplined process — so organisers get qualified buyers and delegates get real outcomes. No guesswork, no passengers.

①

Source & qualify. We identify candidates from our verified East African network and screen each one for purchasing authority, budget, and genuine sourcing intent.

②

Prepare & match. Delegates are briefed and trained, with matchmaking profiles built and pre-scheduled meetings arranged before they travel.

③

Coordinate on the ground. Visa support, logistics, and live event support keep every delegate on schedule and in the right rooms.

④

Report & follow up. After the event we track meetings held and outcomes, closing the loop for delegates and organisers alike.
