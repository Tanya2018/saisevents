Who should register

The hosted buyer programme is for decision makers at companies that buy plastics machinery, materials or packaging.

  • Plastic product makersPipes, containers, household goods, furniture and construction products.
  • Food and drink packaging companiesPET bottles, milk and flour pouches, woven bags, cups, tubs and closures.
  • RecyclersCollection, washing, granulating and pellet production.
  • Factory owners planning new linesOwners, directors, production and procurement managers.
  • Importers and distributorsResins, masterbatch, additives, plastic machinery and moulds.
  • Manufacturers that use plastic partsAutomotive, electrical, agriculture, cosmetics and pharmaceuticals.

What you will find at AFROPLAST

  • Plastic machineryInjection moulding, blow moulding, extrusion, film blowing and bag making machines.
  • Recycling equipmentGranulators, crushers, washing lines and regrinding equipment.
  • Moulds and partsMoulds, mould components and accessories.
  • Raw materials and additivesPolymers, masterbatch, additives and auxiliary materials.
  • Rubber machineryRubber processing machines and testing equipment.
  • Packaging, printing and non-wovenPackaging machinery and materials, printing and converting, non-woven machinery.
  • Auxiliary equipmentAuxiliary machines and spare parts.
