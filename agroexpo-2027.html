Who should register

The hosted buyer programme is for owners and decision makers at companies that buy farm machinery, inputs or agricultural technology. Buyers cannot be exhibitors or sponsors at AGROEXPO.

  • Commercial farms and growersFlower farms, horticulture, grain, tea, coffee and fruit farms.
  • Agribusinesses and cooperativesFarmer groups, outgrower schemes and SACCOs buying for members.
  • Agro-processors and dairiesMilk, meat, grain, fruit and vegetable processing.
  • Poultry and livestock farmsFeed, housing, milking and veterinary equipment.
  • Machinery dealers and importersTractors, implements, irrigation and greenhouse systems.
  • Agro-input distributorsSeeds, fertilisers, crop protection and animal health products.

What you will find at AGROEXPO

  • Tractors and farm machineryPlanting, harvesting, ploughing, spraying and drying machinery.
  • Greenhouses and climate controlStructures, shading, heating, cooling, fogging and computerised control.
  • IrrigationAutomatic irrigation, pipes, valves and water tanks.
  • Seeds, fertilisers and crop protectionSeeds, seedlings, plant nutrients, integrated pest control and sprayers.
  • Livestock, dairy and poultryFeed, milking and dairy machinery, coops, feeding and watering systems.
  • Agri-tech and energyAgricultural robotics, cold storage, packaging, solar and biogas.
