## Stock photos

All stock photos are free photos from Unsplash (not Unsplash+), used under the Unsplash Licence: free for commercial use, no permission or attribution needed. Licence: https://unsplash.com/license

They are served from Unsplash's image CDN (images.unsplash.com) with `auto=format`, so browsers get WebP or AVIF at the size they need. The site's build tools could not download them directly, so they are not stored in this repo. Self-host them later by downloading each image and replacing the URL.

None of these photos were taken at a SAIS Events delegation or at the named expos. Expo cards that use one say so in a note above the expo list.
