Common questions

What happens after I apply?

Our team reviews your details and contacts you on your email or WhatsApp within 24 hours. We share the next steps and agree a time for your consultation.

Does membership guarantee a hosted buyer place?

No. We prepare your profile and help with the application. The organisers alone decide hosted buyer approvals.

Does SAIS Events organise the expos?

No. We are delegate and exhibitor sourcing agents. We work with organisers and coordinators and prepare delegates to get the most from each event.

Can I choose more than one expo?

Yes. Choose every expo you may attend. You can also tell us about an expo that is not on the list.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your details are used only to support your registration and matching.
