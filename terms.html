Terms and conditions

By using SAIS Events coordination services, you agree to the terms below.

  1. Our role

    SAIS Events is a coordination partner. We identify suitable expos, prepare and submit your application, and follow up with the organiser on your behalf. We are not the event organiser and we do not make selection decisions.

  2. Coordination fee

    Our coordination fee is set by mutual agreement between SAIS Events and the client. The amount and payment terms are agreed before any work starts. The fee pays for our coordination work. It is not a payment for approval, sponsorship or a place in any programme.

  3. No guarantee of approval

    SAIS Events does not guarantee direct approval for:

    • Hosted buyer or VIP buyer programmes
    • Joint venture programmes
    • Paid, sponsored or partly sponsored trips

    Selection is made only by the event organiser, based on their own criteria. These include your professional profile, company import and purchasing volumes, product interests, country, available spots and budget. The organiser's decision is final and cannot be appealed, by you or by SAIS Events.

  4. Benefits are set by the organiser

    Flights, hotel, passes and other benefits differ for each buyer and each event. Coverage may be full, partial or none. Only benefits confirmed in writing by the organiser apply. SAIS Events does not promise any benefit on the organiser's behalf.

  5. Costs not covered

    Any cost the organiser has not confirmed in writing is paid by the participant. This includes visa fees, travel insurance, meals, local transport and personal expenses. If you book flights or a hotel outside the organiser's official process, the organiser may refuse to refund them.

  6. Accurate information

    All company and personal details you give us must be true and complete. If an organiser rejects, cancels or charges you because of false information, you carry that cost. SAIS Events may also stop working with you.

  7. Your obligations as a hosted buyer

    If approved, you must follow the organiser's rules. These usually include attending for the required number of days, checking in with the organiser, completing the minimum number of pre-scheduled meetings, and not using the meeting platform to sell your own services. If you do not meet these rules, the organiser may withdraw your benefits and bill you for them.

  8. Cancellations and no-shows

    If you cancel or fail to attend after the organiser has paid for flights or a hotel, you must repay the organiser in full. SAIS Events is not responsible for these charges.

  9. Passport and visa

    You are responsible for holding a valid passport and getting the right visa. We can assist with invitation letters and guidance. Visa decisions are made by the embassy or immigration authority, and we do not guarantee a visa.

  10. Personal and non-transferable

    An approved place belongs to the named person. A replacement from the same company needs the organiser's approval, and any extra cost is paid by the client. Where several people from one company apply, organisers normally cover one representative only.

  11. Event changes

    Organisers may change dates, shorten, extend or postpone an event. SAIS Events is not liable for losses caused by these changes or by events outside our control.

  12. Use of your details

    You agree that we may share your company and contact details with event organisers to process your application. Organisers may publish your name, position, company, country, products and website in their event materials.
