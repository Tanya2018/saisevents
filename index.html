Hosted Buyers Programme

Why join a hosted buyer programme?

Tailored networking

Meet directly with industry leaders and peers relevant to your business.

Pre-scheduled meetings

Secure one on one meetings with exhibitors that match your business needs.

Exclusive access

Where the organiser offers them: dedicated lounges, matchmaking services and invite-only networking events.

How it works

  1. ApplySubmit your Hosted Buyer application.
  2. Get matchedWe curate a list of relevant exhibitors based on your needs and buying priorities.
  3. Confirm meetingsLock in one to one meetings in advance so your time onsite is focused and productive.
  4. AttendTravel, join your scheduled meetings and explore the show. Guided tours are offered at some shows.

Who qualifies?

Senior decision makers and professionals with real purchasing power in the show's sector, from food and agribusiness to plastics, metals, apparel and environmental technology. Each organiser sets its own criteria.

Selection and benefits are decided by each event organiser. See our terms and conditions.

  • Qualified buyer sourcing. Importers, distributors and procurement teams with real buying plans.
  • Exhibitor recruitment for selected shows.

  • Hosted buyer application coordination, from shortlist to submission.

About SAIS Events

Started in Nairobi. Working across Africa and the Middle East.

SAIS Events began in 2018. We kept meeting buyers with real budgets and no clear route into the world's big trade programmes, so we built that route.

Since then we have built an international network that connects business delegates, buyers, exhibitors and global exhibition organisers. Today we work from Nairobi, Kenya, with an office in Dubai.

Our mission is to make international business opportunities more accessible by connecting qualified companies with the right exhibitions, suppliers, buyers and business networks.
