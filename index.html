What we do

For expo organisers

Tell us who you need. We send you a vetted shortlist and get them to your show.

  • Delegates and exhibitors screened for authority, budget and real buying intent
  • Matchmaking profiles built and meetings booked before they travel
  • Invitation letters and visa paperwork handled, so they actually arrive
  • A report after the show on meetings held and deals started

We get you onto the right expo programmes, prepared and ready to do business.

  • Hosted buyer applications prepared and sent to the organiser

  • Official invitation letters and visa guidance

Who we are

Started in Nairobi. Working across Africa and the Middle East.

SAIS Events began in 2018. We kept meeting buyers with real budgets and no clear route into the world's big trade programmes, so we built that route.

Today we work from offices in Nairobi and Dubai.
