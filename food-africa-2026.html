How SAIS Events handles it

Questions buyers ask us

Straight answers.

Is the matchmaking programme really necessary?

It is the whole point of going. Registration gives you the seat and the hotel. Matchmaking is where your meetings get booked with the suppliers you actually want. Buyers who skip it walk the halls on their own and come home with business cards instead of orders.

Why do I need a code to open the hosted buyer page?

We screen buyers before they reach the form. It keeps our delegation list clean with the organiser, and it means the applications we send through get accepted. Ask Kate or Veronica and we will issue yours.

I do not have a company website or profile. Can I still apply?

Not without one. It is mandatory. We build company profiles and simple websites for buyers who need them, so email or WhatsApp us and we will sort it before your application goes in.

What does SAIS Events charge me?

Tell us on WhatsApp and we will give you the figure before you commit to anything. There is no charge for finding out whether you qualify.

Can I bring a colleague?

The funded seat is issued to one named decision maker. A second person can travel with the delegation at their own cost, and we handle their booking and invitation letter too.

Am I obliged to buy anything at the show?

No. You are obliged to attend the meetings booked for you. Buyers who skip their appointments are dropped from future editions, so we only put forward people who will show up.

What if I only get the hotel package?

You still get three nights in a five-star hotel, all transfers and a full meeting diary. You pay only for the flight, which we will price for you before you decide.

Is Food Africa worth the trip for an African buyer?

The last edition drew over 500 buyers from 71 countries. Cairo sits right between Africa and the Middle East, and it is co-located with Fresh Africa and interpack Middle East Africa, so produce, packaging and processing suppliers are in the same halls.
