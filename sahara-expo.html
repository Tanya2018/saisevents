Hosted Buyer Programme — The Gold Standard

Qualifying delegates may be elevated to the Hosted Buyer Programme — Sahara's invitation-only tier. Past hosted buyers received complimentary round-trip flights, 3 nights hotel, airport transfers, VIP lounge access, and pre-scheduled meetings with senior agribusiness decision-makers. Over 450 meetings were held and $1.9M+ in international deals were closed in the last edition alone.

Special Programmes & Conferences

Exclusive empowerment sessions for delegates

As part of your delegation, you'll have access to curated empowerment programmes running alongside the main exhibition. These are not generic add-ons — they're targeted sessions for agribusiness professionals.

🎤 Sahara Conference

High-level agribusiness conference featuring ministers, government officials, and industry leaders from Egypt, Africa, and the MENA region. Real policy. Real connections.

💡 Sahara Seminar Powered by AHDO

Hands-on professional development seminars co-powered by AHDO, covering modern agriculture, sustainability, and capacity building for African agribusiness operators.

👩‍🌾 The Women in Agri Networking Event

A dedicated empowerment forum for women professionals in agriculture — networking, mentorship, and leadership across the Africa-MENA corridor.

🇨🇳 Zhejiang's Agricultural Workshop

Exclusive deep-dive on Chinese agricultural innovation and technology transfer opportunities — especially relevant for East African importers and farm technology seekers.

🌍 Agribusiness Trade Opportunities Forum

EU-Africa and cross-border agribusiness trade session. Strategic entry points, import/export frameworks, and live deal facilitation for serious operators.
